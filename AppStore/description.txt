Exhibit Trail — A calmer museum visit

PLANNED LISTING DRAFT — not a released app. Review against implemented features before publishing.

Bring your own floor plan. Choose what you want to see. Keep your impressions private.

Exhibit Trail is being developed as an offline iPhone companion for museum and gallery visitors with limited time. The planned workflow lets you import a map photo or PDF page, pin a personal shortlist, choose an order, and keep the next exhibit close at hand.

Planned features:
• Mark stops as must-see, interested, or bonus.
• Pace a visit against your own departure target and estimated room-transition buffer.
• Check exhibits off with undo and record your impressions.
• Attach photos you select, without library-wide access.
• Export CSV summaries and versioned JSON backups with chosen attachments.

Your visit data is intended to stay on your iPhone. No accounts, cloud sync, advertising, indoor beacons, or location tracking are planned.

The pacing display is an estimate based on your inputs, not navigation, confirmed opening hours, or a guarantee that you will reach every exhibit. Use floor plans you have permission to import and share.

Standard native iPhone app planned. Native iPad support is disabled. Dual-screen presentation is a future design target, not an available feature.
